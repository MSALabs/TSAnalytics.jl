## scripts/ — 20 generators and comparators

Every number in every handoff came from one of these. `gen_fixtures.R`
and `gen_coint.R` regenerate the synthetic CSVs bit-for-bit;
`ref_*.R` / `ref_py_*.py` regenerate the JSON; `cmp.py`, `cmp_irf.py`
and `eg_fixed.R` reproduce the comparison tables in the handoffs; and
`verify_irf_se.py` reruns the two independent checks of the analytic IRF
standard errors (~1 minute).

Copy `fixtures/*.csv` to `test/fixtures/` and `scripts/*` to the
matching `test/verification/<item>/` directories, so a future session
can regenerate every number rather than trusting a JSON file.
