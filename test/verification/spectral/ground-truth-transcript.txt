Bug found and fixed during verification:
`_modified_daniell_kernel`'s vector-argument method divided its input
by 2 internally, but `spectral_density`'s own call site had *already*
divided `spans` by 2 (matching R's own external `spans %/% 2` before
calling `kernel("modified.daniell", ...)`) -- a double-halving bug.
`spans=[3,3]` degenerated to the trivial kernel `[1.0]`, making the
"smoothed" density bit-identical to the raw periodogram. Fixed by
having the vector method treat its input as already-halved. All values
above (including previously-broken df/bandwidth) now match exactly
after the fix.
