=== Why loglik matches exactly: the sigma2-threading derivation ===

GaussianSSM's own kalman_filter concentrates an overall scale sigma2_hat
out of the likelihood analytically (valid because R*R' is the only
state-noise source and there's no separate H). TimeVaryingSSM's general
filter does NOT concentrate anything (matching statsmodels' own default
behavior, confirmed above) -- Q_t/H_t are the actual variances directly.
Feeding TimeVaryingSSM the RAW R*R' (Q=1) would NOT reproduce
GaussianSSM's concentrated loglik in general (verified: F differs by
exactly sigma2_hat when done this way -- a real, derivable fact, not a
bug). to_time_varying(ssm, y) instead runs ssm's OWN filter first to get
sigma2_hat, then scales Q_t=sigma2_hat and the known initial covariance
P0=sigma2_hat*Q0_stationary by the same factor. Algebraically: scaling Q
and P0 by a constant c leaves a_t/v_t/the Kalman gain K_t completely
UNCHANGED (K_t's numerator and denominator both scale by c and cancel),
while F_t scales by c -- substituting c=sigma2_hat into the direct
(non-concentrated) log-likelihood formula reduces it algebraically to
EXACTLY GaussianSSM's own concentrated formula. Confirmed both
algebraically (in to_time_varying's own docstring) and by the 364-case
run above (loglik diff = 0.0 or floating-point noise on every case).
